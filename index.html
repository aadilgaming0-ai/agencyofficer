Calculation Reference
Calculation Logic
WRP = No. of Policies × ATS.  WCP = WRP × Credit% per product slab.
Self FYC = Σ (ATS × FYC%) across all self-sourced policies.
MPI % applied on Total FYC based on combined WCP tier: 30% (≥0.5L), 60% (≥1L), 65% (≥2L), 75% (≥5L), 85% (≥10L).
Fixed ₹0.02L payout added for every AO with any business in the month.
L1 Activation Bonus: 1→0.02L  |  2→0.05L  |  3→0.09L  |  4→0.12L  |  5→0.15L  |  6→0.18L  |  7+→0.21L
